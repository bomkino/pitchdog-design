# pitch.dog Brand Foundation

## Governing character

Create work that feels exact but alive, warm but unsentimental, witty but unshowy, generous but not vague, and confident enough to stop. The subject owns the room; pitch.dog supplies the attention.

The foundation is a set of authorities and relationships, not a universal look:

- real work before claims;
- one dominant event and at most one quiet interruption per authored scene; dense tools use clear task hierarchy;
- space that creates pressure, pace, anticipation, relief, or value;
- strong hierarchy with humane detail;
- project worlds allowed to remain different;
- care proven in access, truth, credits, fallbacks, and finish.

## Authority map

- Typography: resolve the current governed source through [typography authority](typography-authority.md). Never cache its values here.
- Digital theme and icons: use [Light, Dark, System and Phosphor](digital-theme-and-icons.md).
- Colour and material: use [semantic colour and the material boundary](colour-and-material.md).
- Pitch.dog websites: use the [Website v12 design language](website-design-language.md).
- Space and sizing: use [spacing, grid, and sizing](spacing-grid-and-sizing.md).
- Motion: use [motion and interaction](motion-and-interaction.md).

Current user decisions and current project authority outrank this document.

## Composition

Give one object authority. Build contrast through scale, placement, interval, edge, density, and silence before adding colour or movement.

Useful tensions:

- large field / small event;
- proof / quiet metadata;
- hard edge / imperfect line;
- ordinary object / one impossible behaviour;
- controlled composition / one human interruption.

Do not turn these tensions into a library of scenes. A different subject should produce a different silhouette.

## Proof and authorship

Let work, products, pages, data, people, process, and testimony carry the claim. Keep proof close, large enough to inspect, and intact.

Protect:

- creator and collaborator credits;
- exact project title, role, date, and source;
- approved crop, colour, aspect ratio, and sequence;
- privacy, rights, and permissions;
- honest gaps when proof is unavailable.

An illustration is a complete composition, not a mascot parts bin. Preserve its native field, negative space, scale, and contextual marks.

## Controls and navigation

Use familiar semantics, clear labels, persistent focus, generous targets, exact state, and recoverable errors. Character comes through hierarchy, language, spacing, and one relevant turn—not hidden navigation or novelty controls.

In digital Brand Mode, Phosphor is the interface icon family. A pictogram supports the action; it does not replace an ambiguous label.

## Internal products

The operation and output lead. Keep productive interfaces Body-led according to the active type-system roles. Use compact density only after touch, reading, and error-recovery needs are met.

Do not make a team member cross a campaign page to use a tool. Brand appears in type authority, proportion, language, status, humane states, and finish.

## Completion checks

- A new person understands what this is and what to do.
- Real proof earns important claims.
- One subject and one event remain dominant in authored scenes; dense tools retain a clear task hierarchy.
- The artifact survives grayscale and motion-off.
- Relevant phone, wide, Light, Dark, System, and reduced-motion states are authored.
- Typography resolves to an immutable type-system source.
- Project colour and proof remain intact.
- Removing decoration does not remove the idea.
- Another studio could not simply replace the logo and publish it.
- Exact checks and unresolved gates are reported without promoting them to acceptance.
