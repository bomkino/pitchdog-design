# External-brand adapter

Use pitch.dog's standard of attention without making the other subject wear pitch.dog's identity.

## Recover authority

Inspect, in order:

1. current brand guidelines, tokens, source files, and accepted artifacts;
2. exact content, proof, audience, and medium;
3. newer owner decisions;
4. project-native visual material;
5. references as structural clues, never surface commands.

If rules are incomplete, derive a project profile from the subject. Do not quietly fill gaps with pitch.dog defaults.

## Preserve authorship

Keep the target's:

- typefaces and fallback rules;
- palette and contrast logic;
- theme and icon authority;
- voice, names, and accepted copy;
- marks, assets, crops, and permissions;
- cultural and material context;
- established interaction patterns where useful.

Do not import the pitch.dog type system, palette, dogs, proof frames, page grammar, or material language merely because this skill is active. For new digital work, author Light, Dark, and System-default states from the target brand's accepted values. If the authority lacks a mode, label the derived state provisional until owner acceptance; if it explicitly forbids one, preserve the conflict and ask. Preserve an accepted icon family. When none exists, Phosphor is a provisional system choice until owner acceptance. Keep texture intrinsic to approved client art, but obey the skill-wide no-additive-grain boundary.

## Transfer judgment

- Clarify the real audience outcome.
- Put proof near claims.
- Find one native tension or governing idea.
- Use hierarchy and relationship before decoration.
- Let material decisions arise from the subject.
- Make interface states humane and recoverable.
- Make motion causal and restrained.
- Author each relevant size or format.
- Inspect the artifact, not the promise of completion.

## Stress test

Ask whether a new logo could replace the current one without changing the artifact. If yes, the design is generic.

Then ask whether removing every pitch.dog-associated surface cue destroys the quality. If yes, the skill leaked.

Family resemblance should remain in care, rhythm, proof, usefulness, and finish—not palette or composition.

## When target branding is weak

Do not “rescue” it with house style. Build a small project-specific system from:

- audience and use context;
- subject matter and proof;
- existing recognizable assets;
- one visual thesis;
- semantic tokens;
- medium-native behavior;
- accessibility and production limits.

Document what is accepted, inferred, provisional, and unresolved.
