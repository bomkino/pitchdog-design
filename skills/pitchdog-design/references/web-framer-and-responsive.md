## Page job and argument

Define:

- who arrives and what they know;
- what they fear, misunderstand, or need;
- what should become clear;
- what proof earns belief;
- what they should feel and do next;
- what belongs on another route.

Build the argument before selecting components. A useful spine may move through orientation, proof, central transformation, offer/method, human or operational trust, practical questions, and next action. Do not impose a section count.

Every principal route can be a first entrance. Give it a page-specific proposition, relevant proof, its own arc, a suitable next step, and an authored phone edition. Shared foundations create family resemblance; they must not clone Home.

## Copy and hierarchy

Favor one locator or eyebrow, one memorable headline, one plain explanation, then proof or action. A semantic turn and its practical benefit should be the same thought.

Give one object authority per view. Avoid a giant headline, animated object, ticker, hovering cards, and several equal calls to action competing at once.

## Proof and density

Put real work, product, data, people, or the actual tool near the claims it supports and at inspectable scale.

If proof is missing:

- use honest named placeholders only in internal drafts;
- reduce or hide the section;
- never manufacture public work to satisfy a layout.

Air comes from clear local grouping, scale contrast, controlled reading measure, and purposeful chapter breaks—not empty viewport acreage.

Use full bleed selectively. Separate scene, wide proof, editorial core, and reading measure. On large displays, enlarge proof and relationships; do not stretch sustained text.

## Components

Share behavior when one authority materially helps:

- shell and navigation;
- buttons and controls;
- form fields and states;
- proof frame;
- FAQ or status row;
- testimonial note;
- case-study shell;
- repeated CMS object.

Keep a composition page-specific when its meaning comes from one narrative or unique choreography. Never begin with a universal section component.

## Responsive authorship

Phone is an edited edition. It may change order, density, proof sequence, crop, overlap, sticky behavior, controls, and interaction model while preserving argument and truth.

Tablet is neither wide phone nor narrow desktop. Test all authored canvases plus in-between widths from the grid reference.

Do not require hover. Avoid squeezed desktop pinning, truncated testimony, hidden legal truth, or a forty-screen stack created by collapsing columns.

## Framer adapter

Prefer Framer's native systems:

- Pages and Layout Templates;
- Stack, Grid, Fill, Fit Content, percentages, `1fr`, aspect ratio, min/max, and px constraints;
- Components and Variants;
- Colour and Text Styles;
- Sticky, Scroll Sections, Scroll Variants, and Scroll Transforms;
- Forms and CMS;
- native effects.

Use `1rem = 16px` at Wide, Desktop, Tablet, and Phone. Use REM for font size, EM or percentage for leading, native tracking controls, and px spacing tokens. Give every text-bearing reusable component explicit Desktop, Tablet, and Phone typography.

Do not translate CSS `clamp()`, `calc()`, `min()`, `max()`, `ch`, or rem/em frame widths into fake Framer canvas instructions. Translate accepted intent into native controls.

Use code components only for approved behavior native Framer cannot reliably deliver, such as measured collision handling, estimator logic, or a real tool engine. Do not build a parallel React site inside Framer.

Inspect Preview and published behavior, not canvas alone. Keep built, rendered, checked, accepted, and published separate.

## Internal tools

Start with the operation, data, form, or output. Use familiar controls, compact but readable density, exact status, direct error recovery, undo where valuable, and restrained motion.

Brand through hierarchy, type roles, paper/ink/status colour, humane language, and finish. Do not make the team cross a campaign page to use a tool.

## Verification

- unfamiliar reader can state what this is, why it matters, and what to do;
- proof arrives early and supports nearby claims;
- grayscale and motion-off hierarchy survives;
- keyboard, focus, touch, zoom, and reduced motion work;
- phone and 4K feel authored;
- loading, empty, error, success, and interruption states work;
- client proof remains untinted and correctly credited;
- links, console, layout shift, crops, and performance are checked in the real browser.
