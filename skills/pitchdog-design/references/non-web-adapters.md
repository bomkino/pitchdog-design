# Non-web adapters

Carry the standard of judgment across media; never make every medium imitate a website.

## Decks and presentations

- In Brand Mode, resolve typography from the canonical type-system non-web guidance; do not rebuild its role system from screenshots.
- Find the argument before the slide system.
- Give every slide one job and the sequence a causal spine.
- Let writing and design move together.
- Give proof enough scale for the room.
- Use project-native visual language.
- Make the ask and handover unambiguous.
- Do not fill a decorative slide quota or turn every slide into a card.

## Documents, proposals, reports, invoices, and rate cards

- Use the active brand's governed typography source and record any substitution.
- Make sustained reading, wayfinding, evidence, footnotes, numbers, and terms recoverable.
- Use alignment and spacing to reduce anxiety.
- Keep warmth in order, headings, and microcopy.
- Put jokes nowhere near money, rights, cancellation, or legal consequence unless clarity remains perfect.
- Preserve exact figures and source notes.

## Identities

- Begin with subject, audience, tension, promise, and material world.
- Define stable roles and variable behaviors.
- Build enough range for distinct expressions.
- Test recognition in real uses, not only a logo sheet.
- Do not make each identity resemble pitch.dog.

## Invitations and personal artifacts

- Begin with the people and occasion.
- Let one personal truth carry the design.
- Make date, time, place, access, RSVP, and contact unmistakable.
- Use tenderness and humour naturally.
- Do not turn the occasion into studio advertising.

## Print and editorial

- Resolve font handoff, roles, and known metadata caveats through the active typography authority.
- Let paper, ink, crop, fold, binding, margin, sequence, and production limits create real tactility.
- Confirm trim, bleed, colour profile, resolution, overprint, and proofing requirements.
- Do not simulate web motion or fake physical distress.

## Social and campaign assets

- Give each asset one clear idea.
- Let a series share logic without becoming identical templates.
- Author platform crop, safe area, duration, caption, and alt text.
- Let project colour lead when appropriate.
- Do not over-brand or follow platform fashion after the message disappears.

## Functional products

- Use familiar patterns when they reduce effort.
- Give the operation or output the room.
- Make search, status, errors, recovery, permissions, and undo exact.
- Bring character through hierarchy, language, spacing, and finish.
- Do not reject usable product grammar merely because it resembles software.

## Translation test

For every translation, state:

1. What meaning must remain stable?
2. What behavior belongs to the original medium only?
3. What material or interaction is native to the new medium?
4. What evidence proves recognition and usability survived?

Translate principle and outcome. Do not scale or paste surface.
