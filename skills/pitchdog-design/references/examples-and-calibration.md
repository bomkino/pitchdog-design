# Examples and calibration

These are reasoning mechanisms, not reusable copy or page templates.

## Semantic turns

A strong line lets one familiar phrase perform two useful jobs: name the situation and expose the benefit. Keep the practical meaning immediately nearby. Never copy an example into unrelated public work.

Mechanisms:

- fresh scrutiny versus overfamiliar material;
- future possibility paired with present evidence;
- an industry phrase moved into the artifact's medium;
- programme status made part of the offer;
- scope or testimony allowed to determine form.

## Visual theses

A thesis is a decision filter, not public copy. Examples of mechanisms:

- the page behaves like an argument coming into focus;
- work occupies the room while the studio stays quiet;
- one project remains central while collaborators accumulate around it;
- current status determines route and action;
- a note determines the measure;
- scope visibly determines price.

Reject any thesis that can be pasted onto every project.

## Page calibration

- **Portfolio/work:** proof in the first viewport; project worlds stay distinct; archive reads like an index, not filters for their own sake.
- **Case study:** problem, decisive idea, system, and finish become inspectable; avoid rigid challenge/solution/results boilerplate.
- **Pricing:** make the estimator or scope logic the meaningful interaction; no scroll spectacle needed.
- **Contact:** begin open; form or direct route arrives before a sales performance.
- **Testimonials:** let exact words and attribution determine space; no equal cards or star theatre.
- **Programme:** show current status before a click; never cosmetically open a paused offer.
- **Internal tool:** operation and output lead; brand appears through exact hierarchy and states.

## Counterfactual calibration

A strong skill can produce:

- Brand Mode with no motion;
- Brand Mode as a dense dark interface;
- Mindset Mode with a bright client palette and sans-serif identity;
- a pitch.dog artifact with no cards or dogs;
- an invitation with warmth but no website colours;
- a plain legal page where humour would be harmful;
- a surgical critique of an already strong artifact;
- a portfolio route honestly withheld because real work is missing.

## Failure calibration

Treat these as failures even when polished:

- pitch.dog-coloured dashboard that is slow to operate;
- client page using Neco and blush without authority;
- long transformation that reveals no new information;
- 4K view with tiny proof and stretched body copy;
- tactile surface that collapses without grain;
- mobile composition that is only stacked desktop;
- monochrome design whose hierarchy depends on colour;
- brand page that claims care instead of demonstrating it.
